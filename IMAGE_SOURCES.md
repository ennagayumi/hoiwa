## Production base photographs

`public/images/base/*` and the product photos added with them (`magnesium-sulfate-heptahydrate-crystal.jpg`, `magnesium-sulfate-heptahydrate-granule.jpg`, `kieserite-granule.jpg`, `kieserite-color-granule.jpg`, `magnesium-sulfate-anhydrous-granule.jpg`, `magnesium-sulfate-powder.jpg`, `magnesium-oxide-powder.jpg`) were taken at the Tianjin production base and published on the producer's own sites (xyfertilizer.com, tjxingyu.com). They are used with the producer's permission. Each image was cropped so that no factory name, logo, signboard, phone number, or watermark remains. Keep that rule for any photo added later.

Next.js Image Optimization serves supported browsers AVIF or WebP derivatives. No competitor assets, third-party logos, or website screenshots are used.

## Production base text

The production-base figures, quality steps and shipment records on the home page, and the magnesium sections of the careers primer, are taken from the producer's own published pages (about, inspection certificates, application and news on xyfertilizer.com and tjxingyu.com). On the products page, the kieserite producer-standard and refined 99% monohydrate grades, the Fe / As / heavy-metal limits, the 4–7 mm heptahydrate size, the container loading guide and the extra MgO grades come from the producer's product pages; the grades backed by our own COAs were left as they were. The producer's pages disagree on annual output (120,000 t, 150,000 t and 300,000 t appear on different pages). The producer confirmed 120,000 t, the figure in its own data sheet, and that is the figure shown.
